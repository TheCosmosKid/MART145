What Is Action & Adventure?

Action and adventure stories focus on exciting events, dangerous situations, exploration, and characters attempting difficult goals.

Common Elements

Heroes and Villains

Many action and adventure stories involve characters working against an opposing force.

Quests and Missions

Characters may have a specific goal that requires them to travel, solve problems, or overcome challenges.

Exploration

Adventure stories often take characters to unfamiliar locations where they encounter new challenges.

Common Features

  • Heroes
  • Villains
  • Quests
  • Adventure
  • Exploration
  • Conflict
  • Challenges
