The Start to Gaming

Gaming has been around for since the 1950's, with the first "video game" was made in 1950 known as "Bertie the Brain" made by Josef Kates which was basically an game of tic-tac-toe. However the first major game released for entertainment was "Tennis for Two" created by William Higinbotham in 1958.

The Rise of Arcade Games

Around the begining of the 1970's was the start of the arcade video game industry from the prexisting arcade game industry. The first commercial arcade video game was "Computer Space" made in 1971 by Nolan Bushwell and Ted Dabeny. Another early coin-operated arcade video game was "Galaxy Game" by Bill Pitts and Hugh Tuck in 1971.
